### EDP (Engineering Design Process)
Since this is the first blog entry, I am at the 1st step in the **EDP**. The first step is: **Define the Problem**. Basically we need to find a problem that my _RNG_ app can possibly answer. One major problem that players find (and myself) from playing an RNG game, is that it gets repetitive after long periods of time. My main purpose of my app is that when you play my game, there is a chance you need to solve an **SAT** problem to continue to the next level. The users can learn skills that help them boost SAT thinking. This solves the repitition & finds a way to make it education-friendly! I want to implement that feature in my app.
